# Project 4: Ice Puzzlele

![Godot Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Godot-4.7-blue?logo=godot-engine&logoColor=white)
![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-PC%20/%20Web-green)

**Developed by:**
- **Kaleb**  *Lead Programmer and Designer*

**Ice Puzzlele** is a sliding ice puzzle game inspired by the ice caves and gyms of the Pokémon series (Gold/Silver, HeartGold/SoulSilver, and Black/White/2). Once you step onto the ice, you keep sliding until you hit an obstacle, so every move has to be planned. The game includes six hand-designed levels with increasing difficulty, plus a new **random level generator** that builds a fresh puzzle each time you play.

---

## How to Play
- **Play in Browser:** [Click here to play on itch.io](https://razk777.itch.io/ice-puzzlele)
- **Source Code:** [GitHub repository](https://github.com/razkosu/p-3)

**Controls**
- **Arrow Keys / WASD:** Move in a direction. On ice, you slide until you hit a rock, wall, or solid ground.

**Goal**
- Reach the exit of each level. Plan your path carefully, because you can't stop or turn mid-slide.

**Modes**
- **Levels:** Six hand-built puzzles that ramp up in difficulty.
- **Random Puzzle:** A procedurally generated ice map. *(See Known Issues: generated maps are not yet guaranteed to be solvable.)*

## Known Issues

- **Generated puzzles may be unsolvable.** The random level generator creates valid-looking maps, but it does not yet verify that the exit is reachable. A solver that checks (and regenerates) maps is in progress.
- **No background music yet.** A background music track is planned for a future build.
- *(Low priority)* Player-adjustable settings for the generator (e.g., map size, obstacle density) are planned but not implemented.
